Cancer Free Hygiene

Want a cancer free hygiene? In today's world with so much mis-information it may be seen as fighting an uphill battle. But there are a few companies out there that have made it their top priority to provide us with truly chemical free hygiene products.


Far too many products are sold today containing harmful chemicals (like: fluoride, aluminium, sodium laureth sulphate, chlorine, etc.) that tend to cause cancer or other dis-eases of the body over prolonged exposure. By learning about the dangers of such products, we can make wiser decisions the next time we shop. Cancer free personal care is possible, and along with an honest list of ingredients of a product we will know whether or not it's safe. Information about each chemical and rating of products has come from a trusted non-biased source, GoodGuide.com. Unfortunately, not all of these products are available on Amazon, so not all of the products below are clickable.
