Solutions

Something is wrong with the world.

Click here to watch the number one and only problem in the world.

Click here to watch one brilliant synopsis of why the world is the way it currently is today.

Wars destroy innocent lives, the media spreads fear and propaganda, bankers and traders manipulate the markets, lawyers and police forces make a killing from injustice meeting "quotas," politicians and lobbyists ignore our opinions, the "privileged class" (2% owning 50% of world's wealth) gets to go to expensive private schools, barely interact with ordinary people, marry within their circle, live in exclusive, luxurious, gated communities far from commoners, and don't have to be held accountable for their actions, placing them above the law... while many of our fellow humans are zombies wandering in soulless shopping malls looking to buy objects that will make them "happy" on their time off on the weekends.

We call this normal?

1. Become as Self-Responsible & Autonomous as possible...

Grow your own food, produce your own energy, do your own work, and educate yourself (outside of school) so that you may come up with your own conclusions instead of living according to the decisions of others.

It's no mistake that the modern educational system for the most part contributes to producing broken, compliant, unambitious, obedient consumers, and office workers afraid to take risks. They are largely meant to produce factory workers for the industrial revolution... not free-thinkers and entrepreneurs for today's growing society.


Cinema, radio, TV, computers, shopping malls, etc. serve only two purposes: to keep us distracted / numb and to increase the profits of the already rich.


When we sit passively in front of the TV or purchase useless junk, we slowly lose the desire or ability to resist lies, injustice, and oppression.


Also, either learn about changing your fiat money into precious metals and store them safely so they don't depreciate, or place it someplace where it's likely to grow the most, or try to bank locally instead of supporting the primary banking monopolies.

2. Understand what real "voting" is...

Understand that real "voting" comes from where you spend your money. Boycott the banks and business monopolies of the "Old World Order" that you feel don't do any good for the whole of society. Why pay over 100 dollars for a pair of shoes that were produced in East-Asia for a handful of dollars by slaves enduring inhuman conditions? Prefer honest companies and local products!


Don't look towards politicians or anyone else to solve your problems for you... you have to solve them.

"If elections changed anything, they would be forbidden!" - Tucholsky

3. Stop thinking in narrow compartments...

In other words, start to notice the similarities between what people stand for and say... opposed to the differences. If not, then we will forever be stuck fighting each other, when for the most part we all want the same things... which can't be realized through division. Set a precedence for others on how to act.

4. Study Psychology!

Learn the "16 Myers-Briggs Personality Types" (click here to watch) so that you can have a better understanding of your fellow human beings. Show consideration for others and develop emotional intelligence for when dealing with interpersonal relationships. Be apart of a happy, healthy, and supportive community.

5. Don't fall into the trap of labeling...

Deceit occurs when you pre-judge something to be "good" or "bad" without studying every aspect of it and coming up with your own conclusions. Living with the decisions of others never gets us to move forward.

6. A healthy mind requires a healthy body...

Get active! Too much reading without physical activity causes an imbalance. As well, eat more organic and non-gmo fruits, vegetables, and *grass-fed / pasture raised* animal products. Drink clean *filtered water too. Not only will you be supporting local farmers, but the less sugar and artificial sugar products you purchase, the less "vote" they have to continue to exist. The less "vote" the pharmaceutical cartels get, since you won't be as sick too.

7. Develop courage in the face of neutrality

Re-watch these movies and think about them: Matrix, V for Vendetta, and Network.

Now think about how dangerous neutral people are in a society, where someone stands by as another gets robbed, where nothing ever changes and injustices are seen as normal...

8. Consider where you want to go...

Think in terms of reason and merit earned through hard work, talent and competence... rather than money and status through family connections. Can you honestly say that today every human being is seen as unique and given the full support of the state to unlock their maximum potential, regardless of status, sex, race, religion, family background, wealth, or anything else that may divide humans?


Today there are over 7 billion people on this planet... and yet about 6,000 old men are in charge, that passively do nothing to help others outside of their circle. How on Earth does this make any sense?


Instead of the current capitalistic democracy that has enviably failed to equally help every class, study "Meritocracy" and the "Venus Project." The goal that I think we could all agree on, is that one day society should look more like "Star Trek," then how it does today if we really want to grow.

After all why should one's motivation be to earn money to just "survive," when their motivation should be to improve themselves so that they may be able to improve the world around them?

9. Focus on...

Instead of regretting the past, or worrying about what may happen in the future... why not take action now for something you believe in?
