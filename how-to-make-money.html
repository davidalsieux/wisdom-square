How to Make Money

This page is in response to a number of emails I receive asking: "How can I make money?"


My response:


I will do my best to point you in the right direction and be around for you to talk to. Before thinking about "getting rich," I would recommend you think about how you could increase your streams of income.



I have to say right now I feel the best opportunity to gain financial freedom is to start a blog online that will help you collect emails from people to stay in touch within a particular niche. Having a blog and email list is by far the best way to grow trust and a brand for your "business." This is what I'm currently focusing on. Of course it's going to be very hard work, and if you're up to it the opportunity is there for you to take. 


People can get rich from the craziest ideas you would least expect after all. This list is one example proving that:http://www.cnbc.com/id/43768333 

If you haven't already, I suggest you read this article I wrote a while ago to get you a bit more familiar with an "online business": secrets-to-owning-an-online-business.html. Also, the best websites that I use right now to help get ideas from are socialtriggers.com and smartpassiveincome.com. They're both incredible resources to help you out, since they've both become very successful online and now teach others how to do the same. I only started my website from scratch about 11 months ago in December of last year, so I wouldn't be able to speak from experience like those two other websites can. 



So you know having an online business is a very long-term thing, you're going to constantly hear that. But I truly believe it's possible to earn any amount of money online, if you remain true to your cause.




After you go through all that I've gave you to look at and take the time to think about it, you can contact me again with anymore questions and I'll try my best to help you again.
